What is a QR Code?

Understanding Quick Response codes from scratch

Definition & Function

A **QR code (Quick Response code)** is a type of two-dimensional matrix barcode that can be read easily by digital devices like smartphones. It stores information as a series of pixels in a square grid.

Where Are They Used?

  • Digital Payments: UPI, wallet transfers at retail shops.
  • Websites: Directing users to web links without typing URLs.
  • Restaurant Menus: Digital menu viewing.
  • Tickets & Passes: Boarding passes, event access.
  • Product Info: Tracking ingredients, manuals, and authenticity.

Why QR Safety Matters

Never trust a QR code blindly

The Hidden Risk

A QR code itself is just an image containing data (usually a link). Your phone cannot tell if that link leads to a safe website or a malicious scam page before opening it. Attackers exploit this blind trust.

Where Can a Malicious QR Take You?

Phishing Web Pages Fake Payment Portals Malware Downloads Fraudulent UPI Flows

Common QR Scams

Recognize fraudulent tactics used by cyber criminals

A Fake QR Codes & Tampering

What it is: Attackers paste malicious QR stickers over genuine merchant payment codes at stores.

How it works: You scan to pay the shopkeeper, but the money goes directly to the scammer's account.

Action: Always confirm the store owner's name on your payment app before entering the UPI PIN.

B QR Phishing (Quishing)

What it is: Phishing emails or messages containing QR codes instead of regular web links to bypass email security filters.

How it works: Scammers claim your bank account or utility service is blocked and ask you to scan the QR to verify details.

Action: Never scan QR codes sent via unexpected emails, texts, or social media messages.

C Fake Payment & Reward QRs

What it is: Scammers send messages saying "You have won cashback/reward! Scan here to receive money."

How it works: When you scan and enter your UPI PIN under the pretense of "receiving" money, funds are debited from your account.

Action: Remember: **You never need to enter a UPI PIN to RECEIVE money.**

D Fake Donation & Event QRs

What it is: Fraudulent disaster relief, charity, or event registration drives using counterfeit QR codes.

How it works: Exploiting emotions or urgency to collect unauthorized funds.

Action: Verify official charity channels and domain names before donating.

Safe QR Payments

Best practices for UPI and digital wallets

Golden Rule of UPI

A UPI PIN is ONLY used to send money. You never need a PIN, password, or OTP to receive money from anyone.

Payment Verification Checklist

  • Verify the recipient name matches the merchant or person.
  • Check that the payment amount is correct.
  • Use only authorized, trusted banking and payment applications.
  • Review transaction summary screens before clicking confirmation.

Warning Signs

Red flags to watch out for

Urgent payment requests with threats
"You won a cash prize / lottery" claims
Misspelled website domains in URL preview
QR stickers placed on top of original shop stands
Requests for OTP, UPI PIN, or bank passwords
Prompting to install unknown remote-access apps

Do's & Don'ts

Quick habits for secure digital engagement

DO's

  • Verify QR code source
  • Check recipient name & amount
  • Inspect physical QR stickers
  • Use trusted payment apps
  • Keep your OS and apps updated

DON'Ts

  • Scan unknown QR codes blindly
  • Share OTP, UPI PIN, or passwords
  • Trust unexpected prize/cashback QR offers
  • Install apps from unverified links
  • Ignore suspicious URL redirects

Real-Life Scenarios

Common situations and correct responses

Scenario 1: The Tampered Shop QR

Situation: You visit a local tea stall and scan the payment QR sticker pasted on the counter.

Risk: Money goes to a fraudster's account instead of the vendor.

Warning Sign: The sticker feels like paper pasted over a plastic stand, or the name on the payment app doesn't match the shop owner.

Correct Action: Ask the vendor to confirm their name or use their verified phone number.

Scenario 2: The Courier Scam Message

Situation: You receive an SMS: "Your parcel couldn't be delivered. Scan QR to pay ₹10 re-delivery fee."

Risk: Phishing site theft of banking details.

Warning Sign: Unsolicited message from an unknown number with a short-link QR.

Correct Action: Delete the message immediately and check tracking directly on the official courier app.

Emergency Guidance

What to do if you encounter a fraudulent QR scam

Immediate Steps if Scammed

  1. Stop Interaction: Close any malicious browser tabs or payment apps immediately.
  2. Contact Bank / Wallet: Call your bank's official helpline to block UPI IDs, cards, or accounts immediately.
  3. Report Cybercrime: File a formal complaint at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
  4. Preserve Evidence: Take screenshots of transaction IDs, messages, and QR codes encountered.
